# Cheeko Website Design System

## Direction

Cheeko should feel like a warm kid-tech product: playful, tactile, bright, and trustworthy for parents. The site should not feel like generic SaaS, generic AI purple, or a childish toy catalog.

## Positioning Language

- Primary: `A playful AI companion, without handing them a phone.`
- Mobile headline: `Meet Cheeko`
- Mobile subhead: `A playful AI companion without handing them a phone.`
- Supporting idea: Cheeko is phone-free, curated, kid-safe, and made for children ages 4-12.

## Color System

- `cheeko-yellow #ffc400`: primary brand field, playful background, highlight surfaces.
- `cheeko-gold #f8a900`: depth, shadows, secondary yellow accents.
- `cheeko-orange #ff7900`: promo bar, emphasis, warmth.
- `cheeko-pink #f95c9b`: main CTA, launch energy.
- `cheeko-cream #fff7e8`: soft card surface.
- `cheeko-card #fffaf0`: hero/product card surface.
- `cheeko-ink #24160f`: primary text.
- `cheeko-brown #6d4a32`: secondary text.
- `cheeko-line #eadcc8`: borders and separators.

## Typography

- Display: Baloo 2 for playful, rounded, brand-forward headings.
- Body: Nunito for parent-readable explanatory text.
- Avoid overly large mobile hero text that competes with the product card.

## Hero Rules

- Keep the child/device photo emotional and prominent.
- Use a warm scrim so text remains readable.
- Desktop card should be compact, similar to the wireframe, not a giant modal.
- Mobile card should sit below headline, with reduced padding, two-column feature pills, and smaller copy.
- CTA must have strong pink fill with dark text, never white-on-white.

## Mobile Rules

- Mobile hero headline should introduce the product first with `Meet Cheeko`, then explain the phone-free promise as supporting copy.
- Product card max width should fit comfortably inside 375px screens.
- Product card padding should be 16-20px on mobile.
- Feature pills can wrap into two columns or compact rows.
- Email input and CTA should remain at least 44px tall.
