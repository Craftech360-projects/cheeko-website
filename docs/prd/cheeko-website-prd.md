# Cheeko Website PRD

## 1. Product Positioning

**Approved positioning:** A playful AI companion for kids, without handing them a phone.

Cheeko should be presented as a dedicated kid-friendly companion device: playful enough for children, reassuring enough for parents, and more purposeful than giving a child a general-purpose phone or tablet.

## 2. Primary Audience

- **Primary buyer:** Parents buying for their child.
- **Secondary buyer:** Gift buyers, including relatives and family friends.
- **Child age range:** Broad kids range, approximately 4-12 years.

## 3. Product Understanding

Cheeko is an AI companion device for kids. It can talk, listen, play, and respond through a device experience built specifically for children. It includes a screen for visual feedback and supports experiences such as games, radio, content cards, AI cards, and curated content playback.

The site should avoid calling Cheeko "screen-free" because the device includes a screen. Instead, it should emphasize being **phone-free**, **kid-safe**, **curated**, and **purpose-built**.

## 4. Design Research Direction

### Reference Inspiration: Chibaree Education App For Kids

The Chibaree reference uses a playful education-product language with bright colors, large visual blocks, feature previews, app-like moments, friendly characters, and child-focused activity storytelling.

For Cheeko, the learning is not to copy the reference, but to adopt a similar level of intentional storytelling:

- Explain features through visual moments rather than plain lists.
- Make each section feel like a small chapter in the child-parent journey.
- Use playful shapes, rounded cards, sticker-like labels, large imagery, and warm colors.
- Pair playful visuals with parent-trust messaging around safety, language, content, and phone replacement.
- Balance kid energy with product credibility so older kids and parents do not read it as a baby toy.

## 5. Visual Direction

### Current Wireframe Direction

The provided landing page uses a bright yellow-to-pink playful palette, large lifestyle photography, rounded cards, the Cheeko fox character, device imagery, testimonial cards, and a simple purchase/early-access path.

### Recommended Visual Personality

- Warm, playful, and high-energy.
- Soft toy-like surfaces and rounded cards.
- Parent-trust sections that are calmer and easier to read.
- Occasional illustrated or sticker-style elements for delight.
- Motion should feel gentle and purposeful: soft entrances, card wiggles, CTA press states, and marquee motion for the promo bar.

### Design Risks To Watch

- Too much yellow/pink may reduce readability and feel less premium.
- Some text blocks are currently small and low-contrast.
- The hero communicates emotion well, but the core promise should be clearer above the fold.
- Several sections repeat similar copy; each should have a distinct purpose.
- The site needs stronger parent-trust messaging before asking for purchase or email.

## 6. Page Structure And Section Requirements

### 6.1 Promo Bar

**Current design:** Orange strip at top with repeated message: "Sale 20% offer on promo FIRSTUSER".

**Purpose:** Create urgency and make the promo code visible immediately.

**Requirements:**

- Display a repeating sale message or marquee.
- Highlight `20%` and `FIRSTUSER` with stronger weight.
- Ensure it remains readable on mobile.

**Suggested improvement:** Make the copy cleaner: "Launch offer: 20% off with code FIRSTUSER". If animated, keep motion slow and respect reduced-motion preferences.

### 6.2 Navigation

**Current design:** Logo, nav links (`Features`, `Cards`, `Try Demo`, `Reviews`), cart icon.

**Purpose:** Help parents jump to core proof points and purchase intent.

**Requirements:**

- Include Cheeko logo.
- Include links to Features, Cards, Try Demo, Reviews.
- Include cart icon with accessible label.
- Use sticky or top-fixed behavior only if it does not cover content.

**Suggested improvement:** Improve nav contrast over the photo and add a mobile hamburger or compact nav. Consider changing `Cards` to `Content Cards` for clarity.

### 6.3 Hero Section

**Current design:** Full-width lifestyle image of child and Cheeko device, with an overlay product signup card.

**Purpose:** Create emotional connection and capture early-access emails.

**Requirements:**

- Use a warm lifestyle hero image showing child + Cheeko device.
- Include product card with `Coming 2026`, `Cheeko Pro`, short description, feature pills, email input, and CTA.
- CTA: `GET EARLY ACCESS`.
- Feature pills: `15+ languages`, `Cognitive Boost`, `100+ Contents`.

**Approved positioning to include near hero:** "A playful AI companion for kids, without handing them a phone."

**Suggested improvement:** Add a visible hero headline and subheadline, not only the product card. The parent should understand the promise in 3 seconds.

### 6.4 Meet Cheeko

**Current design:** Yellow section with text on the left and lifestyle image on the right.

**Purpose:** Introduce what Cheeko is and establish the emotional product story.

**Current copy:** "CheekoAI is a magical AI-powered companion that talks, listens, plays, and grows with your child. No screens, just pure learning fun!"

**Required correction:** Remove or rewrite "No screens" because Cheeko has a screen.

**Suggested copy direction:** "Cheeko is a magical AI-powered companion that talks, listens, plays, and grows with your child, all in a device made just for kids."

### 6.5 Product Options

**Current design:** Two product cards with device images: Cheeko Pro and Cheeko Basic.

**Purpose:** Introduce product variants and pricing.

**Requirements:**

- Show Cheeko Pro price: `₹ 4,990`.
- Show Cheeko Basic price: `₹ 3,990`.
- Include buy CTAs for each.
- Clarify what differs between Pro and Basic before final implementation.

**Open requirement:** Need product comparison details.

**Suggested improvement:** Add 2-3 bullet differences per model. Parents need to know why Pro costs more.

### 6.6 What Cheeko Can Do

**Current design:** Three feature cards: Routine, Imaginative Play, Creativity.

**Purpose:** Translate Cheeko capabilities into real child-life use cases.

**Requirements:**

- Show at least three use-case cards.
- Each card should include image, title, and short benefit copy.
- Current titles: `Routine`, `Imaginative Play`, `Creativity`.

**Suggested improvement:** Make each card more specific:

- `Daily Routines`: reminders, morning/night routines, habit nudges.
- `Imaginative Play`: stories, roleplay, pretend adventures.
- `Creative Learning`: prompts, quizzes, music, language practice.

### 6.7 Language Section

**Current design:** Cheeko device image, speech card, language selection buttons.

**Purpose:** Communicate multilingual interaction and mother-tongue support.

**Requirements:**

- Headline: `Cheeko Speaks Your Language`.
- Explain that kids can talk to Cheeko in their mother tongue.
- Show a UI moment with conversation/speech feedback.
- Include language chips or examples.

**Suggested improvement:** This is a major differentiator. It should become one of the strongest trust and emotional sections, especially for Indian parents and regional-language households.

### 6.8 App / Parent Control / Companion Cards

**Current design:** Pink section with fox character, app store badges, mobile UI mockups, parent approval, verification, offline play.

**Purpose:** Show ecosystem and parent safety controls.

**Requirements:**

- Explain companion app if one exists.
- Show parent control and safe-space verification features.
- Explain offline capability: `Play Anytime, Anywhere`.
- Use app mockups if final product includes a parent mobile app.

**Suggested improvement:** Split into clearer sub-sections or cards:

- `For kids`: talk, play, explore.
- `For parents`: control, approve, monitor.
- `For anywhere`: offline-friendly experiences.

### 6.9 Age Range Section

**Current design:** Pink section titled `A companion for every stage of childhood` with age groups 3-5, 5-7, 7-10.

**Purpose:** Make Cheeko feel relevant across age ranges.

**Requirements:**

- Show age bands and examples of value per band.
- Align with broad 4-12 positioning.

**Suggested improvement:** Update age bands to support 4-12 more clearly:

- `Ages 4-6`: stories, simple games, routines, songs.
- `Ages 7-9`: curiosity, languages, quizzes, creative prompts.
- `Ages 10-12`: knowledge, challenges, independent exploration, radio/content.

### 6.10 Reviews / Social Proof

**Current design:** Pink section with metrics and parent testimonial cards.

**Purpose:** Build parent confidence and reduce purchase hesitation.

**Requirements:**

- Headline: `What Parents are Saying`.
- Metrics: `50+ Happy Reviews`, `4.5 Avg Ratings`, `1 hr Screen time reduced`.
- Include testimonial cards with parent name, location, and child age.

**Suggested improvement:** Make testimonials more readable and authentic. Fix spacing/letter issues visible in the wireframe. If claims like `1 hr screen time reduced` are not backed by data, phrase as a testimonial-based result or remove until verified.

### 6.11 Podcast / Cheeko Originals

**Current design:** Orange/pink section with microphone illustration, Cheeko device, `PODCAST`, `Originals`, `Coming soon`.

**Purpose:** Tease original audio/content ecosystem.

**Requirements:**

- Present Cheeko Originals as upcoming curated content.
- Include `Coming soon` CTA or badge.
- Clarify if this means podcasts, stories, radio, or original audio shows.

**Suggested improvement:** Rename from generic `PODCAST` if kids are the audience. Consider `Cheeko Originals`, `Story Radio`, or `Audio Adventures`.

### 6.12 Our Journey

**Current design:** Timeline from idea to launch.

**Purpose:** Build transparency and launch momentum.

**Requirements:**

- Include a simple timeline from concept to testing to launch.
- Use friendly milestone labels.
- Avoid typos such as "ide"; use "idea".

**Suggested improvement:** Make this a trust-builder: show prototype, child testing, parent feedback, safety review, launch.

### 6.13 Footer

**Current design:** Yellow footer with logo, columns, social icons, illustration.

**Purpose:** Provide final navigation and company details.

**Requirements:**

- Include logo and short brand description.
- Include columns for Product, Company, Support, Legal/Contact.
- Include social links.
- Include copyright.

**Suggested improvement:** Add privacy/safety links prominently because this is an AI product for kids.

## 7. Content Principles

- Speak to parents first, but keep the world playful for kids.
- Avoid overclaiming cognitive or learning benefits unless backed by evidence.
- Replace generic repeated copy with specific use-case benefits.
- Use "phone-free" instead of "screen-free".
- Keep safety, privacy, and parent control visible before purchase intent.

## 8. Accessibility And Responsive Requirements

- Text contrast should meet WCAG AA where possible.
- Buttons and tappable items should be at least 44px high.
- Forms need visible labels or accessible labels.
- Cart icon and social icons need accessible names.
- Promo marquee and animations must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Test responsive layouts at 375px, 768px, 1024px, and 1440px.
- Avoid horizontal scroll on mobile.

## 9. Open Questions

1. What are the exact differences between Cheeko Pro and Cheeko Basic?
2. What are all content/card types? Examples: AI card, games, radio, stories, language, routines.
3. Does Cheeko require internet for AI chat, and which features work offline?
4. Is there a parent mobile app? If yes, what can parents control?
5. Which languages are supported at launch?
6. Are reviews/ratings real, placeholder, or target claims?
7. What does `Cognitive Boost` mean in product terms?
8. What safety/verification process exists for child-safe AI?
9. What is the exact launch/pre-order plan for 2026?

## 10. Approval Gates

Before implementation, confirm:

- Final section order.
- Final copy direction.
- Product feature truth and claims.
- Required assets: logo, hero images, product images, app screenshots, card images, icons.
- Whether design improvements should modify the wireframe or only polish it during build.

## 11. Mobile Experience Requirements

The mobile version must be designed intentionally, not treated as a squeezed desktop layout.

### Mobile Principles

- Lead with the positioning and CTA before secondary details.
- Use a compact navigation pattern with logo, menu, and cart/CTA.
- Keep hero content readable over photography with a stronger gradient/scrim where needed.
- Stack cards vertically with generous breathing room.
- Keep CTAs thumb-friendly with minimum 44px height.
- Use shorter text blocks on mobile and avoid tiny captions.
- Convert wide multi-card rows into swipeable or stacked sections where it improves flow.
- Preserve playful energy with motion, but reduce heavy parallax or large movement.
- Use mobile-specific crops if desktop images lose the child/device focal point.

### Mobile Section Treatment

- **Promo bar:** Single-line marquee or compact static offer, no cramped repeated text.
- **Hero:** Full-screen emotional image with readable headline, CTA card below or partially overlapping depending on viewport.
- **Meet Cheeko:** Text first, image second, with clear spacing.
- **Products:** Pro and Basic stacked cards with clear pricing and CTA.
- **Features:** One-column cards or horizontal swipe cards if it feels more playful.
- **Language:** Device image and chat card should stack; language chips wrap cleanly.
- **Parent Trust:** App/control cards stack in a story order: child benefit, parent control, safety, offline.
- **Age Stages:** Three stacked cards or a compact carousel.
- **Reviews:** Metrics in a 3-card stack or 2x grid; testimonials as swipe cards.
- **Originals:** Keep as a bold visual banner, but ensure text is readable.
- **Journey:** Use vertical timeline on mobile instead of horizontal if needed.
- **Footer:** Collapse columns into grouped links with enough tap spacing.

### Mobile QA Checklist

- Test at 360px, 375px, 390px, 430px, 768px, 1024px, and desktop.
- No horizontal scroll.
- No clipped text or hidden CTA.
- Hero image focal point remains child/device.
- Forms are usable with mobile keyboard.
- Sticky nav does not cover content.
- Animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Lighthouse mobile performance and accessibility should be reviewed before final handoff.
